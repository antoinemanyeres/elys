ELYS Boulderloft, Elsässerstrasse 215a, 4056 Basel, Switzerland ("we"). Contact for privacy questions: [privacy e-mail]. This policy follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP) and, for users in the EU, the GDPR.

We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics tools. The app stores only what is needed to keep you signed in and your preferences on your device (browser storage).

3. Who can see what

  • Your climber name, sends and comments are visible to other signed-in members of the app.
  • You can hide yourself from rankings and from the list of climbers who sent a boulder (Profile > Privacy).
  • Your e-mail address is only visible to the gym administrators.

4. Service providers

  • Supabase (database, authentication, photo storage) – servers in Frankfurt, Germany (EU).
  • Vercel (hosting of the app files).
  • Google, only if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Video links open YouTube or Instagram only when you tap them; their own privacy policies apply.

5. How long we keep data

As long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile and all your sends, votes, likes, projects, comments and video links are deleted immediately. Feedback you sent is kept without your name. Backups are overwritten within [7] days.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, export and delete your data at any time: Profile > Privacy > Download my data / Delete my account. For any other request, contact [privacy e-mail]. You can also complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or, in the EU, to your local data protection authority.

7. Changes

We will inform you in the app about important changes to this policy.
